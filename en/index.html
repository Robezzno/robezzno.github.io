04 · About

About me

I'm a self-taught developer based in Spain. I've spent years building tools for myself and for others: from bots that log my workday from a voice note to a restaurant-menu SaaS or an AI-powered Android app ready for Google Play.

I use AI as a copilot to ship faster, but I verify everything I deliver: tests, automated diagnostics and reproducible deployment. My rule is simple: if it can't be run and checked, it isn't finished.

I work in Spanish and English, remotely, across any European or American time zone.
